    This is a collection of 1200 drawings representing museums. Inspired by Pipin barr<br><br>
    
    Museums are ephemeral and fragile constructions exhibiting a unique and inexpressible feature such as a memory or emotion. When using words fails, silently visiting a museum usually succeeds in conveying something. There is often a museum for the lack of better words. A museum can host other museums and multiple museums can assemble in a composite, more complex, installation of museums. Museums are not limited in size and occur where the laws of physics are mostly irrelevant.

    Museums are inspired by the original works of [Pippin Barr](pippinbarr.com), and his [this is what museums look like]). Encourage people to have a look at [his museums]

    I dedicated to work on Museum as soon I got introduced. Building museum as a way to capture ideas. A museum is where captured ideas are kept.
    Ideas are like butterflies. Museums shall be nets. (Épuisette)
    Museums are contagious.
