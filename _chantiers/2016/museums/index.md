These museums are ephemeral and fragile constructions exhibiting a unique and inexpressible feature such as a memory or emotion. When using words fails, silently visiting a museum usually succeeds in conveying something. There is often a museum for the lack of better words. A museum can host other museums and multiple museums can assemble in a composite, more complex, installation of museums. Museums are not limited in size and occur where the laws of physics are mostly irrelevant.

Museums are inspired by the original works of [Pippin Barr](https://pippinbarr.com), and his [this is what museums look like](https://web.archive.org/web/20170511061136/http://www.pippinbarr.com/2015/12/28/this-is-what-museums-look-like/). Encourage people to have a look at [his museums](https://web.archive.org/web/20210815032322/https://www.pippinbarr.com/category/images/)

I dedicated to work on Museum as soon I got introduced. Building museum as a way to capture ideas. A museum is where captured ideas are kept.
Ideas are like butterflies. Museums shall be nets. (Épuisette)
Museums are contagious.
